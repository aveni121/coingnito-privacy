# Coingnito Privacy Policy

Effective: November 10, 2026

Coingnito is a budgeting app that works entirely on your iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information. In short: we don't collect it.

## What we collect

Nothing. Coingnito has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never receive your transactions, account details, bank login, or any information about how you use the app.

## Where your data lives

- **On your iPhone.** The transactions you import, your accounts, categories and settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device.
- **Bank files you import** are read on your device. Coingnito never asks for, sees or stores your bank login. Full account numbers are never stored: Coingnito keeps the last four digits, so you can tell your accounts apart, and a one-way code made from the number, so it recognizes the same account in your next file.
- **Your iPhone's own backup** (iCloud Backup or a computer backup, if you use one) includes the app's data, so it moves to a new iPhone the same way the rest of your apps do. That backup is managed by Apple and by you, not by us.

## Optional features

- **Import folder (off by default).** If you turn it on, bank files you save to iCloud Drive › Coingnito, or to another folder you choose in the Files picker, pass through your own iCloud account. Choosing a folder gives Coingnito access to that folder only, nothing else in your iCloud Drive. Coingnito imports the bank files it finds there, then deletes them from the folder. Turning on Advanced Data Protection in your iCloud settings gives these files end-to-end encryption.
- **Face ID lock.** iOS handles Face ID. Coingnito only learns whether unlocking succeeded, never your biometric data.
- **Weekly reminder.** The reminder is a notification scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
- **"Request Your Bank."** When a file can't be read, this opens your own Mail app with a pre-filled message containing the file's column names, not its transactions. You see and can edit the message, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

## Third parties

Coingnito currently uses no third-party services that receive your data. If paid features are added later, purchases will be handled by Apple through the App Store, and this policy will be updated before that version ships.

## Children

Coingnito does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

## Deleting your data

Settings › Erase All Data deletes every account, transaction and setting from your iPhone. Deleting the app does the same.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
